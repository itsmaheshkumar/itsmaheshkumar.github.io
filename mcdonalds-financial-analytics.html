BUSINESS QUESTION

What does long-run financial performance reveal?

The dashboard brings key annual metrics into one interactive view. Users can compare revenue and earnings, inspect changes in EPS and margins, and explore how profitability evolved across different business periods.

DASHBOARD FEATURES

What can visitors explore?

  • Year-range filters and six headline KPIs, including total revenue, cumulative earnings, revenue CAGR, and EPS.
  • Revenue and earnings trends, annual revenue growth, profit-margin trends, and earnings-growth gaps.
  • Revenue versus earnings scatterplot, correlation, and linear regression summary.
  • Illustrative 2025–2027 revenue and earnings scenarios, alongside the historical series.
  • A Streamlit data-explorer page for reviewing the underlying cleaned CSV data.
METHODS AND LIMITATIONS

Interpret forecasts with care.

The forward curves are illustrative fixed-rate projections: revenue growth is assumed to be 2.4% annually and earnings growth 4% annually. They are not the output of a fitted ARIMA model. Their shaded ranges are approximate and should not be presented as calibrated 95% confidence or prediction intervals.

The cleaned dataset contains years through 2024, whereas the original uploaded financial-statement file ended in 2022. The additional years should be checked against the original annual reports before claiming every record is independently verified.

This is an educational portfolio project, not investment advice.
