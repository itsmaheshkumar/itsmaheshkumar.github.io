HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTO

1. Save your portrait as profile-photo.jpg in this assets folder.
2. Open site-config.js and change photo: "" to photo: "assets/profile-photo.jpg".
3. Upload both files to your portfolio site. The reserved photo frame will be filled automatically.

Recommended: portrait photo, 4:5 ratio, at least 800 x 1000 pixels.
Do not add a photo until you want it public.
