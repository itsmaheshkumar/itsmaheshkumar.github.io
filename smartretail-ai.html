THE CHALLENGE

From sales data to operating decisions.

Retailers need to understand historical sales, anticipate demand, and make replenishment decisions. This project ties those questions together rather than treating a forecast as the final outcome.

The offline analysis used a subset of Walmart M5 sales data: 100 products from store CA_1. Inventory balances, supplier terms, and costs were not present in the source data, so the inventory stage is explicitly hypothetical.

FORECAST EVALUATION

A measurable improvement.

Using a chronological holdout window, the LightGBM forecasting model reduced WAPE compared with a seasonal-naive baseline. Final holdout records were not used to select model hyperparameters.

BUSINESS INSIGHT

Better forecasts don't always mean better decisions.

In the hypothetical inventory simulation, LightGBM produced a lower forecast error but a stronger negative demand bias. Under identical assumed stock and supplier conditions, its inventory policy experienced more shortages.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Always evaluate the operational consequences of a model—not only predictive accuracy.

DELIVERY

Dashboards and an interactive demo.

The project includes Power BI sales, forecasting, and inventory reporting, plus a four-page Streamlit application with a scenario planner.

The public Streamlit application uses independently generated synthetic data, with simple demonstration forecasting methods. It is not a live deployment of the original offline LightGBM model or the original M5 results.

LIMITATIONS

What the results don't claim.

  • Historical listed-price sales value is an estimate, not verified revenue or profit.
  • Offline forecasts were evaluated on a selected M5 subset; results do not establish production performance.
  • Inventory inputs and costs are hypothetical; no actual Walmart savings or stockout reductions are claimed.
  • Real observed sales can be affected by stockouts and may not equal unconstrained demand.
  • Original datasets and model artifacts are not redistributed in the public demo.
